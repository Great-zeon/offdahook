The Master Stylist

25+ years of passion, chemistry & gifted hands.

Shawnese Parker began her hair styling journey over 25 years ago with a singular obsession: understanding why traditional salon techniques repeatedly left textured, coiled, and 4C hair damaged, brittle, or reliant on harsh chemical relaxers.

Instead of relying on sheer flat iron heat or silicone-heavy serums that suffocate the hair follicle, Shawnese dedicated herself to studying hair chemistry and thermal moisture preservation. By pairing therapeutic medical-grade ozone steam with precise tension blow drying and low-heat ceramic sealing, she cracked the code for lasting mirror shine that bounces in high humidity without stripping curl integrity.

Today, Shawnese is revered across Queens, Long Island, Brooklyn, and beyond as the "Crown Whisperer." Clients travel from across the tri-state area because they know their natural curls are treated with the gentleness, honor, and technical precision they deserve.

The Science of Crown Health

“True mirror silk is engineered in the wash bowl, not created by burning hair with 450°F irons.”

Most salon damage comes from stylists attempting to straighten dehydrated textured hair using extreme iron temperatures. At Off Da Hook, we believe hair cannot be forced - it must be hydrated.

By opening the hair cuticle under our specialized ozone micro-mist steamers, pure moisture infuses directly into the hair cortex. When sealed with organic cold-pressed botanicals and ceramic tension gliding, the hair remains silky, featherlight, and completely shielded against reversion.
