Getting Here

Easy access from Queens, Brooklyn & Long Island.

No stressful parallel parking or congested side streets. Here is the easiest way to arrive at Phenix Salon Suites.

Driving & Highway

Direct access off the Belt Parkway (Exit 23) and the Nassau Expressway (Route 878). Located conveniently along Rockaway Blvd right at the Queens / Nassau County border.

100% Free Parking Plaza

Park effortlessly in the massive private shopping center plaza directly outside Phenix Salon Suites. Zero parking fees, zero meters, and zero street parking headaches.

MTA Buses & LIRR

Nearby MTA Bus routes include the Q111, Q113, and Q114 along Rockaway Blvd. Long Island Railroad (LIRR) Gibson and Rosedale stations are only 5 to 7 minutes away by rideshare.

8 Mins From JFK Airport

Visiting New York or stopping during a layover? Off Da Hook is located only 8 minutes from JFK International Airport, making same-day silk presses and protective styles seamless.
