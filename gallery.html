Community Nonprofit Initiative

Annual Princess Chambers Prom Giving Event.

Giving back is at the very core of Off Da Hook Salon Suites. Every year on May 3rd, master stylist Shawnese Parker partners with Princess Chambers Inc. to provide complete prom pampering - from bespoke hair styling to prom dresses, makeup, and confidence building - completely free for approximately 100 deserving young high school graduates facing hardship.

Annual Day of Giving • May 3rd

Empowering 100 Young Queens with Unforgettable Crowns

Prom night is a once-in-a-lifetime milestone. Together with the Queens/Five Towns community, we make sure no young woman has to miss out due to financial hardship. Our suite doors and volunteer hands open wide to create magic.
