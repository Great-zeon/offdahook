25+ Years Behind the Chair

With over 25 years behind the chair, Shawnese Parker has established herself as one of New York's premier authorities in textured hair health, weightless thermal silk presses, and restorative wellness.

Her signature approach is rooted in hair chemistry: understanding that lasting body, mirror shine, and thermal protection cannot come from excessive heat or heavy silicones, but from deep ozone steam hydration, precise tension blowouts, and ceramic heat sealing.

“We are about empowering all women to embrace their hair whether you prefer locs, braids, weaves, twist outs, afros, or silk cuts, embracing your uniqueness while maintaining the health of your crown.”

- Shawnese Parker

Beyond technical mastery, Shawnese is known for her infectious positivity, warm hospitality, and proud LGBTQ+ inclusive private sanctuary where every client is celebrated like royalty.
