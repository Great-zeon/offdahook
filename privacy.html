Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Off Da Hook Salon Suites ("we," "our," or "us"), owned and operated by Master Stylist Shawnese Parker, is located inside Phenix Salon Suites at 25300 Rockaway Blvd, Suite #192, Rosedale / Woodmere, NY 11422. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, appointment forms, and third-party booking systems.

2. Information We Collect

When you browse our website, book an appointment, or contact our suite concierge, we may collect:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, telephone number, and email address.
  • Appointment Preferences: Preferred services (silk press, braids, locs, chemical treatments), dates, hair notes, and consultation history.
  • Transactional Information: Appointment records and booking details facilitated through our verified booking partner, StyleSeat. Note: We do not store full credit card numbers on our local servers.
  • Usage Information: Basic device type, browser information, and referral paths to ensure optimal mobile viewing and page performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide you with a peaceful, personalized salon suite experience:

  • Scheduling, confirming, and managing your private salon suite appointments.
  • Sending appointment reminders, schedule updates, or suite arrival instructions.
  • Responding to customer service inquiries, custom bridal package requests, or house call consultations.
  • Maintaining sanitation, health, and client service records.
  • Improving website loading performance and mobile navigation.

4. Third-Party Services & Booking

We partner with StyleSeat to process online bookings and schedule confirmations. When you book via StyleSeat, your data is processed according to StyleSeat's privacy policies. We also use secure Google Forms for specific career applications and community prom pampering initiatives. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

5. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Our site utilizes modern SSL encryption, and all customer communications are handled through verified, industry-standard channels.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate details, or ask us to delete your consultation records. To make a privacy request, please contact us directly.
