Suite Policies

Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Agreement to Terms

By booking an appointment, purchasing products, or using services provided by Off Da Hook Salon Suites (Master Stylist Shawnese Parker), you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. Please review them carefully before securing your appointment.

2. Appointment Booking & Deposit Policy

Appointments are scheduled on a one-on-one basis to guarantee privacy, personalized care, and zero double-booking:

  • Deposits: Select services require a valid deposit via StyleSeat or confirmed payment link to lock in your calendar slot. Deposits are applied toward your total service balance.
  • Arrival Time: Please arrive on time. Because each suite session is tailored and sanitized between clients, late arrivals exceeding 15 minutes may require rescheduling or an adjustment to your service scope.
  • Cancellations & Rescheduling: We require at least 24 hours advance notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment without penalty. Late cancellations or no-shows may forfeit their deposit in accordance with our StyleSeat cancellation policy.

3. Hair Preparation & Consultations

  • Hair Condition: Prior to arriving for silk press, color, or protective styling services, please ensure previous extensions, weaves, or braids have been thoroughly taken down unless a takedown service was pre-booked.
  • Health-First Philosophy: If hair has severe compromise or breakage, Master Stylist Shawnese Parker reserves the right to recommend restorative treatment protocols (such as ozone steam hydration or bond repair) before applying thermal heat or chemical processes.

4. Private Suite Conduct & Accompaniment

To preserve the calm, sanctuary-like environment of Suite #192 inside Phenix Salon Suites:

  • Please refrain from bringing extra guests or children unless they are also receiving a scheduled service or prior approval was granted.
  • Respect the quiet relaxation of neighboring suite professionals and clients.

5. Product Sales & Returns

All sales of Loc'd & Loaded botanical hair care products (including serums, edge glazes, and spritzers) are final once opened, in accordance with cosmetic hygiene standards. If a product arrives damaged from shipping, please contact us within 48 hours with photographic proof for a replacement.

6. Limitation of Liability

Off Da Hook Salon Suites provides licensed professional cosmetology services adhering to state health standards. Clients are required to disclose any known scalp sensitivities, product allergies, or medical hair concerns during consultation.
